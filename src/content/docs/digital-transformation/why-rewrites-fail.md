---
title: 'Why rewrites fail'
description: 'The big-bang rewrite is attempted repeatedly and fails for consistent'
order: 1
---

The big-bang rewrite is attempted repeatedly and fails for consistent reasons:

- **The old system's behaviour is not documented, it is observed.** Twenty years
  of edge cases live in the code, and every one of them exists because something
  went wrong once. Nobody can list them, so the new system reproduces the ones
  people remembered.
- **The business does not stop.** The legacy system keeps changing during the
  rewrite, so you are chasing a moving target with a smaller team.
- **Value arrives only at the end.** Two years of spend before the first
  benefit, which means the first budget review is an existential threat.
- **Cutover is all-or-nothing.** One date, no rehearsal, no reverse gear.

The alternative is to make modernisation **incremental and reversible**.
