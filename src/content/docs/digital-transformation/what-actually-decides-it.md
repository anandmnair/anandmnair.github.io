Modernisation programmes are rarely lost on technology.

**Sponsorship that outlasts the first setback.** There will be a quarter where
the numbers look bad. Programmes without real sponsorship die there.

**A visible early win.** Something demonstrable in weeks, not a year of
foundational work. Foundations matter, but they do not sustain support.

**The team that will operate it, building it.** Handover to a separate run team
recreates the wall the transformation was meant to remove.

**Honesty about what to retire.** Every estate contains applications nobody
should migrate. Finding them is the highest-return analysis available, and it is
almost never budgeted.
