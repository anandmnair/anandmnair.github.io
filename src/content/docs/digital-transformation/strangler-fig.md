Route traffic through a façade. Move one capability at a time behind it. The new
system grows around the old one until the old one carries nothing and is switched
off.

What this buys you: value from the first capability rather than the last, risk
confined to one capability at a time, a reverse gear at every step, and a team
that learns the domain progressively instead of guessing about all of it at once.

Sequencing that has worked:

1. **A capability with clear boundaries and real pain.** Proves the pattern where
   success is visible.
2. **The high-change capabilities.** Where modernisation pays back fastest.
3. **The core.** Once the team has earned confidence and the tooling exists.
4. **The rest** — including the honest conversation about what should be
   retired rather than migrated.
