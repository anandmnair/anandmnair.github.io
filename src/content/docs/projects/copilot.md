**GitHub Copilot · Skills · Prompts**

First attempt at bringing structure to AI-assisted development: a shared set
of Copilot skills and prompt templates so a team stopped reinventing its own
prompting conventions per developer. It worked well enough to prove the idea
was worth pursuing, and badly enough to show its limits — everything was
locked to one tool.

Deprecated in favour of [AIDLC](/projects/aidlc/), which keeps the same idea
of standardised, guardrailed AI workflows but makes it tool-agnostic — any AI
coding tool, any IDE.
