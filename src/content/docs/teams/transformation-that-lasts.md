Programmes end. What persists is what got embedded.

**Change the defaults, not the exhortations.** A template with the quality gates
built in changes behaviour permanently; a memo asking for quality changes it for
a fortnight.

**Find the people already doing it.** Every organisation has engineers who
already work the way you want. Their teams are the beachhead, and their advocacy
is worth more than any mandate.

**Measure outcomes, not activity.** Tool licences, training hours and migrated
applications are activity. Lead time, change failure rate, and how long it takes
a new engineer to ship are outcomes.

**Expect the trough.** Every transformation gets slower before it gets faster,
because people are learning while delivering. Say so at the start. Programmes
that promise immediate gains lose credibility at exactly the moment they need it.
