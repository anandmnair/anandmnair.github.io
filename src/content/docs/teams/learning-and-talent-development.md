Skills decay, and the rate is currently accelerating. Treating capability
development as an annual training budget is not a strategy.

**Structured onboarding** — a new engineer should ship something real in week
one. Not because the change matters, but because everything they need to learn
is on the path to shipping it.

**Learning paths, not courses.** A named progression from where someone is to
where the organisation needs them, with checkpoints. Courses without a path are
consumed and forgotten.

**Deliberate practice on real problems.** Katas and internal challenges built
from the actual domain, not from generic exercises.

**Teaching as the final step.** Nobody understands something until they have
explained it to a room that asks questions.
