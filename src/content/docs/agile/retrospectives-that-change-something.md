title: 'Retrospectives that change something'
description: 'A retrospective that produces observations is a discussion. A retrospective that produces one owned, dated, specific change is a feedback loop applied to the team'

---

A retrospective that produces observations is a discussion. A retrospective that
produces **one owned, dated, specific change** is a feedback loop applied to the
team itself.

One change, not eight. Eight improvements attempted simultaneously means none
completed, and the next retrospective opens by discussing the same items — which
is how teams learn that retrospectives do not work.

Structured self-assessment against explicit practices works well at scale — the
team scores itself, sees where it sits, picks one dimension to improve, and
re-scores later. It makes maturity visible without turning it into a management
metric, and it gives the conversation something concrete to be about.
