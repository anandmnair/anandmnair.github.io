"Fail fast" is routinely misread as permission to be sloppy. It means the
opposite: **engineer failures to be cheap and visible, so you can afford to
discover them early.**

That requires investment — feature flags, automated tests, fast rollback, canary
releases, monitoring that surfaces problems in minutes. With those, failing fast
is a strategy. Without them, it is just failing, discovered late, in production,
by a customer.

The cultural half matters as much: if raising a problem early is punished, people
will raise it late, when it is undeniable and unfixable. Psychological safety is
not a soft add-on to fast feedback; it is the mechanism.
