Estimates are forecasts, not commitments, and the accuracy of a forecast falls
off sharply with distance. Estimate the next slice carefully, the quarter
roughly, and the year directionally.

**Prefer slicing to estimating.** "How long is this?" is a much harder question
than "how do we cut this into pieces we can ship this week?" — and the second
one produces working software while the first produces a number.

Watch what velocity does to a team. Used to forecast, it is useful. Used as a
performance measure, it inflates within two sprints and stops measuring
anything.
