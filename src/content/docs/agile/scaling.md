Most scaling frameworks add coordination machinery. The alternative that has
worked better for me: **remove the need for coordination.**

Autonomous teams aligned on architecture and contracts need far less process
than dependent teams with excellent process. Which makes scaling agility largely
an architecture problem — bounded contexts, independently deployable services,
contract tests — rather than a process one.

Where genuine cross-team alignment is needed, guilds and chapters have worked
better for me than added ceremony: a technical community that agrees standards
in the open, rather than a governance body that issues them.
