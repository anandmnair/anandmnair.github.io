### Domain-driven design

- *Domain-Driven Design* — Eric Evans. The original. Dense; the strategic
  patterns in the later chapters are the part most people skip and most need.
- *Implementing Domain-Driven Design* — Vaughn Vernon. More practical, better
  starting point.
- *Learning Domain-Driven Design* — Vlad Khononov. The most approachable of the
  three, and the best on subdomain classification.
- Alberto Brandolini's writing on event storming.

### Architecture

- *Building Microservices* — Sam Newman. Honest about the costs, which is rare.
- *Fundamentals of Software Architecture* — Richards & Ford.
- *Designing Data-Intensive Applications* — Martin Kleppmann. The reference for
  distributed data. Worth re-reading yearly.
- Alistair Cockburn's original hexagonal architecture article.
- *Release It!* — Michael Nygard. Stability patterns, and where the resilience
  vocabulary comes from.

### Craft and testing

- *Test-Driven Development: By Example* — Kent Beck.
- *Growing Object-Oriented Software, Guided by Tests* — Freeman & Pryce. The
  outside-in argument.
- *Refactoring* — Martin Fowler.
- *Working Effectively with Legacy Code* — Michael Feathers. Characterisation
  tests, and the only book that takes legacy seriously.
- *Specification by Example* — Gojko Adzic. What BDD is actually for.

### Delivery and organisation

- *Accelerate* — Forsgren, Humble & Kim. The four key metrics, with the research
  behind them.
- *Continuous Delivery* — Humble & Farley.
- *Team Topologies* — Skelton & Pais. Conway's law as a design tool.
- *The Site Reliability Engineering Book* — Google. Free online. Error budgets.

### Ongoing

- martinfowler.com — bliki entries, especially on refactoring and microservices.
- The Morning Paper archive — computer science papers, explained.
- InfoQ architecture track and the QCon talk archive.
