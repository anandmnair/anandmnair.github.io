- **Copilot, not autopilot.** AI writes a great deal of my code now. It owns none
  of it — whoever's name is on the commit is accountable for every line.
- **Boundaries before frameworks.** Hexagonal architecture enforced by tests, not
  by good intentions. A boundary nobody tests has already been crossed.
- **Unblock, then optimise.** A stalled programme is usually blocked on something
  specific and unglamorous. Find it before adding people.
- **Craftsmanship is a team sport.** Standards in one person's head are trivia.
  Standards in a guild, a checklist and a shared library are engineering.
- **Ship the boring parts too.** Failover rates, consistency trackers, checksum
  validation. They are what make a claim into a fact.
- **Reusable beats bespoke.** One library a hundred services depend on produces
  more leverage than a hundred good decisions made separately.
